What Personal Information We Collect

Pharmacy Automated Mechanisms (“PAMCA”, “we”) collects only the information needed to run the Site, sell our products and answer your questions.

Information you give us:

  • Orders and checkout: your name, pharmacy name, email address, phone number and, for delivery, your shipping address.
  • Accounts: your email address and password if you create an account (passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider), plus any delivery details you choose to save to your profile.
  • Contact form: your name, email address, phone number and message.

Payment information: you pay on Square's secure payment page. Your card details go directly to Square — we never receive or store your card number.

Technical information: like any website, our hosting provider receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, and keeps them in short-term server logs used to run and secure the Site. We do not use advertising tracking tools.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

  • Cart cookie (cart_token): set when you first add a product to your cart, so your cart is kept between visits. It expires after 30 days.
  • Browser storage: if you sign in, your session is kept in your browser's local storage. The Site also stores your cart's item count and a copy of the product list there to load faster. A guest order lookup (your Purchase ID and phone number) is kept only until you close the browser tab.
  • Google reCAPTCHA loads only when you start filling in our contact form, to tell people apart from spam bots. Google may set its own cookies when it does. Its use is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • Google Maps: the map on our contact page and footer is provided by Google, which may set cookies when the map loads.

Google Analytics may also set cookies to help us understand how visitors use the Site. We do not use advertising cookies.

How We Use Your Personal Information

  • To process and fulfil your orders: payment, shipping with Canada Post or a courier, or in-store pickup.
  • To send order confirmations and updates, and to let you view, edit, track, cancel or get a refund for your order.
  • To manage your account and saved delivery details.
  • To answer messages you send us.
  • To understand how visitors use the Site, so we can improve it (Google Analytics).
  • To protect the Site and our customers from fraud and abuse (for example, spam filtering and limits on repeated attempts).
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

Service Providers We Share Information With

We share personal information only with the service providers that run parts of the Site for us, and only what each one needs:

  • Square — processes payments and refunds. Receives your name, email, phone, address and the order amount. Square's privacy notice.
  • Canada Post — calculates shipping rates and delivers parcels. Receives your postal code for rate quotes and, for shipped orders, your name, address and phone number.
  • Brevo — sends our emails (order confirmations and updates) and delivers contact-form messages to us. Receives your email address, name and the message content.
  • Supabase — stores our database and customer accounts, hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS).
  • Vercel — hosts the Site and runs its servers.
  • Google — reCAPTCHA and Maps, as described under Cookies.
  • GoDaddy — we use GoDaddy to power our online store. You can read more about how GoDaddy uses your Personal Information here: https://www.godaddy.com/en/agreements/privacy.
  • Google Analytics — we use Google Analytics to help us understand how our customers use the Site. You can read more about how Google uses your Personal Information here: https://www.google.com/intl/en/policies/privacy/. You can also opt out of Google Analytics here: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Legal Compliance: We may also share your personal information to comply with applicable laws and regulations, to respond to a subpoena, search warrant or other lawful request for information we receive, or to otherwise protect our rights.

Where Your Information Is Stored

Our database is hosted in Ireland (European Union), and the Site's servers run in Dublin, Ireland. Some of our service providers may also process information in the United States or other countries. Information stored or processed outside Canada is subject to the laws of those countries, and may be accessible to their courts and authorities.

How Long We Keep Information

  • Orders: kept for as long as we need them for our business, tax and legal record-keeping.
  • Unfinished checkouts (orders that were never paid): deleted after 7 days.
  • Carts: deleted after 45 days without activity.
  • Accounts: kept until you ask us to delete yours.
  • Contact-form messages: kept in our email for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry.

How We Protect Your Information

The Site is served only over encrypted HTTPS connections. Card details are handled entirely by Square. Access to customer information is limited to authorized staff, and the systems that store it are protected by access controls.

Your Rights

You can ask us to give you access to the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it (except where we must keep records by law). You can update your saved delivery details at any time from your profile page. To make a request, email us at sales@pamca.net.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Do Not Track

We don't use cross-site tracking or targeted advertising, so our practices are the same whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes to our practices or for other operational, legal or regulatory reasons. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
