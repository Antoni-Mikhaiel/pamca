Refund & Cancellation Policy

How to change or cancel an order placed on pamca.net, and how refunds work.

Back to Home

At a Glance

  • Add items to an order: within 24 hours of placing it.
  • Cancel for a full refund: within 48 hours of placing it, as long as the order hasn't been completed.
  • Refunds go back to the card you paid with.
  • After that, contact us and we'll review your request.

Cancelling an Order for a Full Refund

You can cancel a paid order and receive a full refund online within 48 hours of placing it, as long as we haven't yet marked the order as completed (fulfilled).

The refund covers everything you paid for the order — products, shipping and HST — including any payment for items you added later. Cancelling an order can't be undone.

Adding Items to an Order

Within 24 hours of placing an order, you can add products to it. Added products are charged at their current price plus HST, and you pay only the difference through Square's secure payment page. Your order is updated once that payment is complete. The shipping charge set at checkout doesn't change.

Products already in an order can't be removed or reduced online. To change them, cancel the order within 48 hours for a full refund and place a new one, or contact us.

We may lock an order against changes earlier while we prepare it. A locked order can still be cancelled for a full refund within 48 hours, unless it has been completed.

How to Edit or Cancel an Order

  • With an account: sign in, open My Profile and find the order. Choose Edit order to add items or Refund to cancel it.
  • Without an account: select Profile in the site menu and use your Purchase ID — the 6-digit number from your order confirmation — together with the phone number you used at checkout.

The Edit order and Refund options only appear while an order is still within its window.

How Refunds Are Paid

Refunds are issued through our payment processor, Square, to the original payment method. The refunded amount is shown on your order straight away; how long it takes to appear on your statement depends on your card issuer.

After the Online Window

After 48 hours, or once an order has been completed, it can no longer be changed or refunded online. Please contact us and we'll review your request with you — including if an order arrives damaged, incomplete or incorrect.
